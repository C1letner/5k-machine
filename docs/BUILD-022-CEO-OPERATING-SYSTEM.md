# Build 022 — CEO Operating System

CEO now has a durable operating snapshot and daily Owner report.

Inputs include research queue health, system-run reliability, universe freshness, hypothesis pipeline, forward experiments, and development work.

CEO classifies company GREEN / YELLOW / RED, records material findings, issues durable directives to executives, and explicitly lists Owner decisions required.

CEO cannot enable real trading, change MEDIUM risk posture, increase capital authority, withdraw funds, or conceal evidence.

Initial cadence: daily operating report. Event-driven escalation can be added after reliability is proven.

This is deterministic management V1. Future reasoning can enrich management judgment, but evidence and authority remain database-enforced.
