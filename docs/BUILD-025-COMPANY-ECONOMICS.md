# Build 025 — Company Economics Ledger

CFO/COO now has a durable financial ledger separate from trading/shadow ledgers.

Cost categories:
- Infrastructure
- AI/model
- Market data
- Software/API
- Execution fees
- Slippage
- Other

Revenue categories:
- Trading P&L
- Interest
- Rebates
- Other

Only VERIFIED items enter verified company economics.

TRUE COMPANY NET PROFIT = verified revenue - verified costs.

Initial known operating items recorded:
- Render Command Center free tier: $0
- Coinbase public spot data: $0

Unknown or future costs must not be assumed zero merely because they are absent. CFO and Auditor should flag unverified/missing economics.

This ledger measures the operating company. It does not grant trading authority.
