# Company Risk Mandate — Draft V0.1

Owner risk posture: **MEDIUM**.

The company exists to produce meaningful positive financial outcomes; it is not designed to minimize volatility at the expense of useful returns.

Interpretation:
- Research may be aggressive and broad.
- Capital deployment must remain bounded and diversified.
- Normal losses and drawdowns are acceptable costs of pursuing return.
- Ruin, uncontrolled leverage, uncapped loss, hidden correlation, and strategy drift are unacceptable.
- Risk limits must scale with forward evidence; stronger evidence may earn larger authority.
- No strategy earns capital authority from historical backtests alone.
- Portfolio-level risk matters more than whether any single trade appears safe.
- Owner-approved risk posture does not yet grant live trading authority. authorizedToTrade remains false until a separate capital mandate is approved.

Future CIO/CEO risk controls should translate MEDIUM into explicit numerical portfolio limits using forward evidence, liquidity, volatility, correlation, and drawdown behavior rather than an arbitrary universal position size.
